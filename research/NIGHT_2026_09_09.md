`M_r1` ran through the night and is not finished. Everything below was done
while it ran, against banked data, without touching the run path — except one
change the user asked for directly, which is recorded as a caveat because it
lands asymmetrically.

## The theme: n=1 stated as architecture

`M_r1` is the second M cell ever run to depth. Every quantitative claim about
arm M moved when it arrived, and three published caveats asserted those numbers
as properties of the arm.

| claim | `M_d1` | `M_r1` | caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| growth rate | 271 tok/turn | **98** | 13, 19 |
| peak tokens by turn 116 | 32,073 | **17,031** | 1 |
| crosses the 24k ceiling | before turn 116 | **~turn 174** | 1 |
| live decisions | 123 | **37** | 16 |
| `DECISION_CAP=40` | binds, 3× over | **never fires** | 16 |

Same arm, same task set, same model, **same seed**, same `max_turns`. Three M
cells have now measured 209.6, 271 and 98 tokens/turn.

The *directions* all survive: M is uncapped, nothing truncates it, and
supersession does not bound it. What does not survive is any threshold sized
from these figures — which is exactly what caveats 6 and 16 were building
toward. The turn at which the treatment starts to bind is not known to within
60 turns.

**Side finding, independent support for caveat 10:** supersession fired 1 time
in 124 decisions for `M_d1` and **0 in 37** for `M_r1`. It cannot be the
mechanism that bounds the durable region, because it barely happens.

## The one that changed a published number

README's `operate`/GLM-5.3 table was measured through a path matcher blind to
`.tsx` and `.css`. Regenerated:

| | published | current |
|---|---|---|
| absolute grounded output | +43.33, p=0.0304 | **+53.11, p=0.0002** |
| grounded rate | −0.104, **p=0.0197** | −0.023 [−0.092, 0.054], **inconclusive** |

`e00e829` taught `PATH_RE` to see those extensions; `src/ground.ts` records that
65.4% of the texts feeding grounding on this cell named a file the old pattern
could not match, so they took the `body === null` branch and were graded
`grounded` unchecked. A had been collecting more of that free pass.

**That deleted the study's only measured precision/productivity trade-off.**
`site`/4.7 puts B ahead on rate (+0.152) and `site`/5.3 is flat (+0.002), both
inconclusive. No cell shows A grounding a larger *fraction* of what it claims.
README no longer says otherwise.

## Two things the metric was hiding

**The free pass is not a uniform tax.** `extract_claims` grades an uncited claim
`grounded`, so an arm is paid for not naming a location. On `site`/4.7 — the
+144 headline — B cites a file on **28%** of its claims against A's **49%**. The
winning arm is the one that named fewer. Restricted to attributed claims the
effect survives at **+16.0 (p=0.0093)**: the direction holds, the magnitude was
89% artifact.

**Hallucination was never reported at all.** On the study's own absolute-output
endpoint, B separates from A on fabricated symbols too: operate +2.4
(p=0.0186), site/5.3 +2.8 (p=0.0039). The *rate* does not separate anywhere
(p=0.15–1.0), so this is not a rate claim. The defensible statement is a ratio —
B's extra output is ~22:1 grounded-to-hallucinated on operate, ~18:1 on
site/5.3. That favours B, which is why omitting it was never necessary.

## Instruments that were passing while measuring nothing

- **`probe/midflight.ts` had no liveness check.** Found by running it against
  `M_r1` while the cell had recorded nothing for 29 minutes: it printed "no
  problems" throughout. Its directive and predicate checks read accumulated
  rows, so they read identically whether the run is working or stopped. Now
  reports staleness and fails past 2× `TURN_BUDGET_MS`, verified with a positive
  control rather than by reading it.
- **It also went blind at the moment a cell failed.** It looked only at
  `status='running'` cells, so a cell that exhausted `MAX_MS` — recording
  `status='done'` with the missing directives simply absent — dropped out of the
  log entirely, and the orchestrator's status test then skips it. The failure
  was what made it invisible. It now lists finished-but-truncated cells;
  verified against the real `M_d1` row, which reports `4 of 6 directives`.
- **`score_revisions` graded 40 runs against the wrong script.** The relay fix
  named relay and left `?? SITE_REVISIONS` standing, so every later set without
  an entry inherited it: rainwater 21, audit 9, build 7, moon 1, ship 1, test 1.
  Now returns one `unscorable` row naming the set. No published A/B cell is
  affected. The missing `ship` patterns are still unwritten on purpose.
- **The credential scan had been reading zero files.** It built its path list
  from `git status --porcelain`, which prints repo-root-relative paths. Re-run
  with a positive control over all 2,491 tracked files: no provider keys. One
  real credential found and removed — `workspace/AM_v3/relay/tmp-state/pc.txt`
  held a live better-auth session token.

## What needs a human

- **`M_r1` and `AM_r1` will not run identical retry code** (caveat 20). The
  fixed 30s poll landed mid-flight, so `M_r1` keeps the old backoff. Nothing the
  model is sent differs and arm signatures are unchanged; the gap is minutes of
  recovery latency per outage. Do not quote wall-clock or stall duration as an
  arm difference.
- **If `M_r1` truncates before turn 200**, it records `status='done'` and the
  orchestrator skips it. `./go.sh drive M_r1` resumes it — but not while another
  cell is live, because parallel arms form one deep provider queue.
- **The `ship` revision patterns** are still undefined, deliberately. A wrong
  pattern is a confident wrong verdict; a missing one is silence.
- **`DECISION_CAP` should not be re-decided yet.** The number it would be sized
  from varies 3× between the only two runs that have measured it.

## What was deliberately not done

Not restarting `M_r1` to equalise the retry code: the benefit is ~5 minutes of
recovery latency, and killing it risks the supervising orchestrator reading the
child exit as completion and advancing to `AM_r1`, abandoning the cell at turn
122.

Not writing the missing metric definitions. Not raising `MAX_MS` while watching
it bind. Not starting a second cell alongside `M_r1`.
